## Follow-up (3–5 days later)
Hi [Name], just circling back in case my earlier note got buried. Happy to send a quick mockup idea for [Business Name] if useful. Let me know.

## Process
1. Research 5–10 local businesses per day (Google Maps, Nextdoor, Facebook, drive-bys).
2. Send 5–10 personalized pitches.
3. Offer a free mockup for serious interest.
4. Close on Starter or Business package.
5. Deliver in 7–10 days.
6. Ask for testimonial + 1–2 referrals.
7. Offer Care Plan.

Track everything in clients.csv.
